# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in `pii-evidence` respectful, professional, and welcoming to contributors of different backgrounds, identities, languages, jurisdictions, and levels of experience.

This project studies privacy-sensitive data. Contributors should treat both people and source material with care.

## Expected behavior

Examples of constructive participation include:

- discussing evidence and technical claims rather than attacking people;
- stating uncertainty explicitly;
- correcting mistakes with source-backed reasoning;
- respecting privacy, licensing, and redistribution constraints;
- avoiding unnecessary reproduction of personal or health information;
- giving credit for research, review, and corrections;
- welcoming questions about language, jurisdiction, accessibility, or domain assumptions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable conduct includes:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language;
- sexualized or demeaning comments;
- doxxing or publishing another person's private information;
- posting real PII/PHI to prove a point when safe synthetic evidence is sufficient;
- knowingly misrepresenting source provenance or review status;
- pressuring contributors to reveal confidential data;
- retaliating against good-faith security, privacy, or methodology concerns.

## Privacy-sensitive discussions

Do not post real customer, patient, employee, or user data in public project spaces. If a report requires sensitive material, use the project's private security-reporting channel and minimize the data shared.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate this Code of Conduct or the repository's security/privacy policies.

Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, project communication channels, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Attribution

This document follows the intent of widely used open-source community conduct policies while being written specifically for the privacy-sensitive nature of this project.
